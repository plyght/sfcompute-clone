These are the canonical requirements SF Compute evaluates suppliers and facilities against. Term sheets and contracts reference a specific published version below.

  • Cluster Requirements cover supply-side hardware: GPU servers, networking, storage, data center, support, and acceptance.
  • Colocation Requirements cover colocation facilities: power, cooling, physical security, connectivity, redundancy, and certifications.

  • Each version has its own URL (/v1, /v2, …) and is frozen on publish.
  • The unversioned link (e.g. /requirements/clusters) points at the current version.
  • Contracts cite the explicit version, so a signed agreement always resolves to the exact text it was signed against.
  • Retired versions stay available indefinitely.
